| Name | Meaning |

So there are two ways to read every number in this document:

- **Literal**: use P/W values as CSS px. To verify with `getBoundingClientRect`, set the viewport to **1542 × 945**, then DOM rect = P − (22, 23). (The baseline was shot at 1586 × 992, which is the PNG size including the matte; that viewport is 2.9 % wider and 5 % taller than the mocked window.)
- **Scaled (recommended)**: CSS px = W ÷ 1.5, i.e. the same scale as the home prototype. Verify at a 1028 × 630 viewport, or keep proportions (section 2.2) at 1280 × 800.

All tables give P px and a "÷1.5" column.

1. `docs/ORANGE_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2 lists "登录页布局与动效" under *不在本次自动改动* ("结构不动"). Prototype 06 changes structure, removes the brand block and adds controls.

**Relation to the home scene: same motif and layout, different painting.** It is not a crop, zoom or blur of the home scene.

Evidence:

- Login vs 404: 80 px patches register at shift (0, 0) with median NCC 0.855 everywhere except the girl. Same painting, girl repainted.
- Login vs home: no single uniform scale + offset explains the image. Best uniform crop NCC 0.79; the best fit needs anisotropic scale (x 0.94, y 1.10), and sub-regions want incompatible offsets (bridge origin (−108, −86), left tree (−8, −82), water (−28, +32)). An affine RANSAC keeps only 15 of 35 patch matches.
- Element proportions differ. The arch opening is as wide as home's (214 px against 211 px at the waterline, each in its own image's pixels) but 1.5× as tall (120 px against 81 px): a near-semicircle here, a flat segmental arch in home. The girl is 1.24× larger (face 26 px wide against 21 px), drawn in more detail, and has a different pose. Home: hands down, small and soft. Login: both hands resting on the railing. 404: left hand on the railing, right arm raised with open palm pointing right, toward the card (hand at x 443..476, y 362..379).
- The left bank is a stone wall with flowering vines in 06/07, a plain embankment in home. Houses are nearer and crisper. The willow's longest strand reaches 36 % of the window height in 06/07 and about 52 % in home.
- 06/07 are crisper and more saturated: mean saturation 22–24 against 15, mean |dx| 4.1 against 2.1.

### 6.3 Route facts for 404

`frontend/src/router/routes.tsx` has no catch-all route. The prototype shows the page without the Dock, so it belongs beside `login` under the root route, outside `ProtectedRoute` and `AppLayout`. The index route already redirects to `/login`, and `LoginView` redirects signed-in users to `/dashboard`.

### 6.4 Cost notes

- Both pages are static: one still image, no WebGL scene, no continuous animation. The baseline's four drifting shapes and the logo float/glow go away.
- One frosted surface per page. Fields and buttons use plain translucent fills, so there is no nested backdrop blur.
- The frosted look can be had with no runtime blur at all by painting the pre-blurred still `scene-business.webp` (23 KB) inside the card at the same crop as the page background, under the glass veil.
- `scene.webp` (242 KB) is already shipped. The decorative crops are small pale washes and should stay under about 30 KB in total as WebP.
- No web fonts are added.
